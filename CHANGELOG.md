# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-13

- First native macOS release of LiteFocus: one current activity and one goal.
- Hover to view, click once to edit, Pin to keep the panel open, and Clear to remove both values.
- Yellow floating ball with a white sprout, draggable placement, Chinese and English interfaces, and Light and Dark modes.
- Local persistence for content, position, and Pin state. No account, sync, history, reminders, or analytics.
